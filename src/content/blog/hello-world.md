I started a blog. Mostly so I stop losing things.

My day job is ML engineering, which in practice means a lot of time on Databricks figuring
out stuff that isn't documented anywhere. I solve some weird platform problem, move on, and
three months later I hit it again and have no idea what I did. So now it goes here.

What to expect:

- **Field notes.** The seams, the workarounds, and the "why does this 401" moments from
  building real things on real platforms. The first one is
  [Pushing the Limits of Databricks Apps](/blog/pushing-the-limits-of-databricks-apps/),
  about building a multi-tenant ML platform on two apps that can't share an auth cookie.
- **ML stuff.** Whatever I'm learning or poking at, some of it distributed, some of it
  just fun.
- **Maybe photos.** I shoot film ([@upafilm](https://instagram.com/upafilm)). No promises.

No schedule. I post when I have something worth writing down.

If any of it is useful, or wrong, or you just want to talk shop, [reach out](/contact/).
I'm also open to new roles.
